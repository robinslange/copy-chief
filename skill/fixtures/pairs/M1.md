# Introducing Grey Lynn Knife Co.

Grey Lynn Knife Co. offers professional whetstone sharpening services.

I sharpen kitchen knives by hand on whetstones in Grey Lynn. Drop your knives
in the box at the shop door by Tuesday and they are back on Friday, sharp
enough to slice a tomato without pressing.

NZ$18 a knife, any length. Serrated knives are NZ$24.

If a knife is not sharper than the day you bought it, bring it back within a
week and I redo it free or refund you.

Book a drop-off at knives.example/drop and quote code TOMATO. You get a text
with your box number within the hour.
