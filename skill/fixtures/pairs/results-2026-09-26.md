variant runs and in at most one base run. None of those four pairs passes the
key in full, because the second clause (other targets unchanged) fails every

scoring step omitted it.

O3 and O4 are uninformative because both already fire on base. That is a
finding about base, recorded rather than edited away:

- O3 fires on base in 2 of 3 runs because the guarantee's standard ("not

- O4 fires on base in 3 of 3 through its narrowed second arm: the Tuesday

  stated). Same id, different arm; the tally cannot tell them apart.

The side effects say more than the targets. P3 becomes a majority finding on
five of six variants whose edit never touched the mechanism line, and O3 and
O4 drop out of variants that never touched them. On this draft those three
checks sit near an even split between runs, so a three-vote majority moves
them in and out. The vote suppresses one-run noise; it does not make a check
that sits at 50% stable. A check whose majority flips on an unrelated edit is
the next thing to measure, per check, across more runs.
