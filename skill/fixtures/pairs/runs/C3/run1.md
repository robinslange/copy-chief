What fails: the steps are out of order and conflict. The first line reads as a walk-up drop ("the box"). The close says you book first and are then given "your box number". A reader acting on the first instruction arrives without a box number. The draft also never gives the shop's address, only "in Grey Lynn", so the reader cannot complete the physical step from the page. Principle: Kennedy. Instructions have to be unambiguous enough that the reader can carry them out from the page alone.

What fails: the opening makes two claims the body never comes back to. (a) The safety claim, that a dull knife is how you got cut, is never paid off. Principle: Hopkins. Every promise made up front is redeemed later, each one separately.
